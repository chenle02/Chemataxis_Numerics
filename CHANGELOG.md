# Changelog

All notable changes to the curated Paper III numerical companion are recorded
here. Historical raw result paths are intentionally stable and are not renamed
between releases.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-07-14

First submission-facing data release.

### Added

- Immutable amplitude-constrained stationary-continuation bundle `v1` with
  four parameter regimes, three meshes, eight signed amplitudes, and 96
  archived states.
- Machine-readable fit summary, indexed NPZ state archive, long-form profiles,
  branch-point table, vector/raster figure, and SHA-256 manifest.
- Schema 2.0 evidence contract that verifies 780/780 acceptance gates, exact
  source revisions, stationary slopes, asset hashes, and provenance-only
  exclusions.
- Reader-facing evidence matrix, reproduction guide, release provenance, and
  citation guidance aligned with the Paper III numerical section.

### Changed

- Promoted the non-minimal `beta=3` and both minimal-model parameter sets to
  validated stationary evidence while retaining their earlier time-dependent
  or coefficient-seeded archives as provenance only.
- Replaced both time-integration manuscript assets with deterministic,
  font-embedded PDF/PNG counterparts.
- Updated the public metadata and citation record to version 1.0.0.

### Evidence boundary

- Two fixed-seed non-minimal time-integration families remain validated within
  their stated semidiscrete scope.
- Four superseded historical families remain versioned but cannot be used as
  reader-facing quantitative evidence.
